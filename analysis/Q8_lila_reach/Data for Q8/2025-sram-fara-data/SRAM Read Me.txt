Food Access Research Atlas, SNAP-authorized Retailer Access Map Data Download
Source: USDA, Economic Research Service using data from various sources as documented on the Food Access Research Atlas Documentation webpage.
Recommended Citation: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Economic Research Service. Food Access Research Atlas, SNAP-authorized Retailer Access Map.
Notes about the Food Access Research Atlas, SNAP authorized Retailer Access Map Download Data:
This file contains multiple spreadsheets:
1. A variable lookup (VL) that provides explanatory names for all Food Access Research Atlas, SNAP-authorized Retailer Access Map, General Tract Characteristics variables.
2. A table of all Food Access Research Atlas, SNAP-authorized Retailer Access Map, General Tract Characteristics Data.
3. A variable lookup (VL) that provides explanatory names for all Food Access Research Atlas, SNAP-authorized Retailer Access Map, Straight-Line (Euclidean-based) Distance variables.
4. A table of all Food Access Research Atlas, SNAP-authorized Retailer Access Map, Straight-Line (Euclidean-based) Distance Data.
5. A variable lookup (VL) that provides explanatory names for all Food Access Research Atlas, SNAP-authorized Retailer Access Map, Driving (Network-Based) Distance variables.
6. A table of all Food Access Research Atlas Data, SNAP-authorized Retailer Access Map, Driving (Network-Based) Distance Data.
Data explanations and data sources are on the Food Access Research Atlas's SNAP-authorized Retailer Access Map Documentation page on the USDA, ERS website.
Notes: Cells are intentionally left blank when data are unavailable or not applicable.
